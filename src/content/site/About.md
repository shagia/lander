How do you make a center out of your influences? Devin S.G., born Devin Joseph Glover from Oakland, CA, has been asking himself that for years and continues to find answers.

Growing up on the internet with computers, cars, trance and anime in rotation can be shared by many, but defining a sound to all of your favorite things altogether can be a difficult play for any incoming artist. While spending time learning in Los Angeles, each thing in the rotation has taken turns being louder than the rest, pushing a sound built on deep expression, tricky patterns, wailing cloudy synths, and hard, groovy drums.

Over time, that rotation has spilled into collaborative sessions, support sets, and the occasional URL night where half of the crowd is the world. He's worked alongside artists Tomu DJ and onlytom, opened for Leonce and ANAMANAGUCHI, appeared on streams like SPF420 and AEL: MetalEvergreen, and ended 2025 with a spot on HATSUNE MIKU Digital Stars 2025.

Alongside his music is a set of engineering and design skills that draw a bigger picture: bombastic techno, computer graphics, and fun multimedia, all meant for today and himself. His center is still growing, but that's always going to be the point.
